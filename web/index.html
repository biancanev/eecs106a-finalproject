Authors: Kush Mahajan, Tim Brugarolas, Ryan Kwong, Gabriel Gallo, Thomas Chang

Project Overview

Modern autonomous systems must intercept and track moving targets in environments that are:

  • Dynamic and unpredictable
  • Partially observable due to obstacles
  • Constrained by limited sensing and actuation capabilities

To achieve this, we seek to Develop a trajectory optimization framework that enables a seeker robot to intercept a moving target. We have constrained the project such that the turtlebot operates under patrial observability:

  • No full map of the environment
  • Incomplete or noisy target state information
  • Ensure real-time replanning as new measurements arrive
