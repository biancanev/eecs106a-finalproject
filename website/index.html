Welcome to our EECS 106A Final Project

Project Overview

The goal of our project was to place two turtlebots in a unknown enviroment with obstacles blocking the path between them. Our job was to implement a way for one of the turtlebots designated as the seeker bot to intercept the target bot, which also could be moving, in optimal time by mapping its surrondings and figuring out the quickest path to the target.

Inspiration

Our work was inspired by current interception research being done as well as missle interception work that is being done where one missle is able to autnomously intercept another. The work and challenges seen in those examples encapsulates many that we encontered along our project such as accurately mapping noisy enviorments and succesfully planning an optimal trajectory.
