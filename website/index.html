Turtlebot Interceptor

Welcome to Team 15's Fall 2025 EECS 106A Final Project

Project Overview

The goal of our project was to deploy two Turtlebots in an unknown environment with obstacles obstructing the path between them. Our task was to design and implement a system in which one Turtlebot, designated as the seeker, intercepts a target Turtlebot—potentially in motion—in optimal time by mapping its surroundings and determining the most efficient path to the target.

Inspiration

Our work was inspired by ongoing research in interception systems, as well as missile interception technologies in which one missile autonomously intercepts another. The challenges observed in these systems reflect many of the difficulties we encountered in our project, including accurately mapping noisy environments and successfully planning an optimal trajectory.
