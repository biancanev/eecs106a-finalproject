Results

Results Overview

Our Turtlebot successfully navigated the test environment using the implemented control algorithms. The robots were able to map their surroundings using LiDAR and computer vision, then plan a path from the seeker to the target. While the resulting path was not always perfectly optimal, it generally made sound decisions about the overall route and, in some cases, performed even better than we had expected.

Early Results

Early tests of our MPC controller demonstrated that while some aspects of the initial design were successful, there was still significant room for improvement. These tests showed that our environmental mapping was largely accurate; however, the controller implementation did not behave as intended, with the robot either completely ignoring obstacles or failing to react appropriately to continue along its trajectory toward the target.

Early 1 - Obstacle avoidance not working.
Early 2 — Trying to avoid obstacles, but still struggling.

Recent Results

After some work on the MPC and implementation, we observed significant improvements in the robot's ability to navigate the test environment. It was able to better avoid obstacles and follow the planned trajectory more closely, resulting in more reliable and efficient navigation.

Recent 1 — Found more optimal path than anticipated by squeezing through cones.
Recent 2 — Actively avoiding obstacles, intercepted target.
